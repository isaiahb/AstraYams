Structure before mechanics

The stem carries nodes and internodes. Branches arise at nodes beside leaf stalks; each compound leaf contains independent leaflets. The flowering regions contain shorter branchlets, reduced leaves and simplified bract-enclosed floral units.

The earlier union-shaped leaf membrane is not reused. This model separates the seven measured leaflet contours; their hidden bases and 3D folds are inferred.

What remains unverified

This is an anatomy model, not a calibrated simulation or a reconstruction of your partner’s plants. Branch density, dimensions, root arrangement and posture are assumptions. Flowers show external structures, not full microscopic anatomy.

No sway animation, cutting event, force response, collision validation or trained policy is represented here. Those tests must use this whole-plant context and organ identities.
