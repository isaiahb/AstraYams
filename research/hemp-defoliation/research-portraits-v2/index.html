REFERENCE SELECTION · RESEARCH PORTRAITS V2

One living plant. A controlled photograph.

Three different specimens and one additional view from the APPF Plant Accelerator hemp study. These are original research photographs, not generated or upscaled images.

What improved: single potted subjects, plain backgrounds, documented specimen IDs, original PNGs and no support cage in the selected portraits.
What remains imperfect: dark foliage, yellow basal tissue, considerable empty background, and cropped pot bottoms in the frontal views. These are useful annotation candidates, not a verified match to a commercial mature canopy.

Best whole-plant candidate

084877-W · Original PNG · 2472 × 3296 · 8.15 MP

One upright, branched specimen with its shoot tip visible, a plain black background, and no support cage. Individual leaf edges and branching are distinguishable.

The pot bottom is cropped; upper leaves are dark; yellow basal tissue is present. This is not evidence of a mature production canopy.
