Control C in Crispim Massuela et al. (2022), Figure 1b, at 65 days after rooted-clone planting. Approximate projected centerlines are manually traced. Some lower connections are occluded; every branch depth is a hypothesis. The working 0.80 m axis scale is an estimate, not a precise ruler calibration.

Reference trace. Orange paths are partly visible axes; dashed blue paths indicate inferred branches. Overlaid paths do not establish the hidden anatomy. Full original figure, dates and scale context.

What changed because of the evidence?

  • Retained a continuous main axis instead of the previous short, arrested stem.
  • Unequal side branches attach at individually specified nodes.
  • Leaf size, inclination, petiole length and leaflet complexity vary by position; depth variants remain hypotheses.
  • Nodes, organs and branch identities persist through the replay.

Canopy dimensions and trunk/internode descriptors follow the measurement approach used by Carlson et al. (2021). That study concerns field-grown hemp; its dimensions are not imported as indoor defaults.
