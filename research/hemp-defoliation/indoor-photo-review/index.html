What to compare with our model

  • Canopy structure: leaf overlap, multiple upper shoots, and exposed lower branches—not just total leaf count.
  • Removal history: leaf removal and clearing lower growth create different visible changes. An “after” photo does not tell us every earlier intervention.
  • Variation: cultivar, stage, training history and neighboring plants all affect silhouette. One photograph cannot define the whole distribution.
  • Physics: photographs can constrain geometry and connectivity. They cannot validate stiffness, friction, cutting force or damage thresholds.
