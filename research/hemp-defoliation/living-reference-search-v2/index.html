Living upright hemp: verified source improvement

The left image is the original unprocessed archive member, displayed by CSS only. It verifies an upright plant with its apical tip, main stem, and pot opening in a single native 2472 × 3296 frame. The pot’s lower edge is cropped by the image boundary. The right image is a different inherited sample/view/time and is included only to make the wider field of view inspectable; no paired lineage is established.

Archive landing page: APPF hemp PIA public S3 listing. Direct archive: side-far 0 / plant 084267-H. DOI: 10.25909/23211779.v1. License: CC BY-NC 4.0.

Shortlist and limits

  1. APPF TPA Hemp PIA, native side_far RGB: only verified high-resolution, public source found that satisfies upright living plant, visible pot opening/main stem, and indoor phenotyping context. It is a research-only CC BY-NC 4.0 dependency and its plants retain cages and a sparse, elongated architecture. The verified frame does not show the pot bottom and plant ID 084267-H has no verified cultivar mapping.
  2. PLOS ONE 2021 Figure 1: a CC BY 4.0, 2250 × 2300 composite that provides dense, flowering/pre-harvest whole-plant examples. It is a paper figure rather than a downloadable image dataset; each plant is only a panel within the composite and pot context is limited. It is an anatomy reference, not a segmentation-data replacement.
  3. Mendeley Data 2025 greenhouse monitoring set: verified whole, front-view potted plants under controlled greenhouse imaging, CC BY 4.0, but released sample frames are only 273 × 552 pixels even though the paper reports 1080p capture. It is unsuitable as the requested high-resolution replacement.
