# Biological behavior audit

The existing v3 model does not yet satisfy the requirement that all biological behaviors be research-justified. Existing source links support selected anatomical concepts; they do not validate the numerical script.

| Behavior | Evidence status | Required disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Leaves and axillary structures connected to nodes | Anatomical support in organ-development-v1/REPORT.md, references 1 and 8 | Retain concept; exact initiation timing remains unsupported |
| Separate stem elongation and thickening | Reference 6 supports distinction in studied hypocotyls | Retain separate variables; do not transfer its ages to every segment |
| Changing leaf form with developmental position | Reference 3 supports association | Replace fixed profiles with source-linked cohort/rank data |
| Transition at node 6 and divergence 145 degrees | Unjustified numerical scenario | No biological claim; requires measurement |
| Bud eligibility restricted to ranks 3–12 | Unjustified spatial restriction | Do not present as biological absence or dormancy |
| Bud activation after 5.2 units, plus branch-order delay | Uncalibrated timer | No physiological claim; requires longitudinal evidence |
| First lateral leaf always three leaflets, then five | Unjustified universal sequence | Requires axis-stage evidence before replacement |
| Main leaves fixed at 1/3/5/7 progression | Simplified sequence, not cohort fit | Do not universalize |
| Every leaf expands over 4.2 units | Uncalibrated shared duration | Separate organ kinetics and obtain evidence |
| Petiole length fixed at 40% of blade length | Unjustified fixed ratio | Requires joint measurements |
| Cotyledons disappear between units 7 and 12 | Unjustified lifespan | Explicit hypothesis; requires dated observations |
| True leaves never senesce | Missing process, not evidence of retention | Restrict study claims; needs aging observations |
| Upward direction correction and decorative blade sag | Uncalibrated geometry heuristic | Not gravity, tropism or elastic validation |
| Fixed segment sizes and radius curves | Uncalibrated geometry | Do not label cultivar dimensions or stiffness |
| Axis caps and 34-unit end | Computational study bounds | Not mature size or biological cessation |
| Enlarged bud markers | Diagnostic overlay | Keep labeled; not organ morphology |

No unsupported rule is promoted by this audit. Legacy output remains an exploratory hypothesis, not a biologically justified growth forecast. Future changes require a per-rule provenance entry containing source, finding, population, supported scope, parameter evidence and limitations. Removing a rule must not silently replace it with a different arbitrary rule.
