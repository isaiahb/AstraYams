Why these parameters exist

Growth changes the architecture, not just the scale.

The greenhouse phenotype dataset provides serial heights for five hemp cultivars. Its height trajectories continue through the reproductive transition and then tend toward a plateau. We retain the observed points and separate them from synthetic organ growth. Anderson et al. (2021), article & S2 data.

The model uses a fixed graph per individual. Internodes extend across a moving growth front, branches expand after their supporting node, and leaves expand at their own attachments. Reproductive proxy expansion has its own timing. No claim is made that these inferred organ histories reproduce the measured individuals.

Indoor Alpine Star CBD research measures shoot length, branch count, leaf length and leaf width separately across observation times. That supports separate model controls rather than deriving everything from height. Islam et al. (2022), Figures 2 & 9.

The short-main-axis families encode a possible prior management history. Greenhouse hemp studies show that altered apical history can change height and side-shoot development. This is a structural scenario, not an instruction or an independently fitted cultivar. Greenhouse morphology study (2026).

Islam et al. (2022), Figure 9, CC BY. Indoor research facility; different experimental groups. No claim that this is the partner’s production crop.
