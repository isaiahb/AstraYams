# High-resolution real reference photographs

Three originals were downloaded byte-for-byte, hashed and visually inspected.
No image generation, upscaling, raster editing or ML inference was performed.

Selected whole-plant candidate: Danielstjohn, Potted marijuana plant.jpg,
3456×4608 displayed orientation (JPEG EXIF rotates the stored raster), CC BY-SA 4.0.
Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Potted_marijuana_plant.jpg
The pot bottom is cropped; background is busy; cultivar/profile unknown.

Indoor alternative: Plantlady223, Two hydroponic cannabis plants.jpg,
3300×2475, CC BY-SA 4.0. Two container-grown plants with shaded lower foliage.
Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Two_hydroponic_cannabis_plants.jpg

Rejected primary candidate: Felix Brönnimann, Cannabidiol (CBD).jpg,
3158×4737, CC BY-SA 4.0. Plants are cropped/occluded and foreground blurred.
Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cannabidiol_(CBD).jpg

No source verifies that these are the partner's cultivar or that they are
phenotyping replicas. Treat them as image-segmentation references. Respect
source attribution and share-alike requirements for distributed derivatives.

The static review supports native-pixel inspection with browser scrolling.
Original files live under images/; sources.json preserves URLs, hashes and
observed limitations. Existing 2472×3296 APPF industrial-hemp reference remains
available under living-reference-search-v2, but depicts a different sparse
plant and has CC BY-NC 4.0 licensing.
