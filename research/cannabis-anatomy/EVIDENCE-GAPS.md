# Evidence gaps and limits

## What remains unknown

| Question | Current evidence | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Is the partner's material represented? | No partner specimens or data reviewed | Literature descriptions cannot establish its particular anatomy |
| How variable are shapes within and between specimens? | Flora descriptions report variation | No representative image dataset or measured distribution assembled |
| What is the three-dimensional attachment geometry? | Descriptive terminology | Hidden connections and depth cannot be recovered from an uninspected or single image |
| How do fresh tissues behave mechanically? | Tissue identities in a microscopy study | No calibrated stiffness, damping, friction, or failure properties established |
| What explains a resting posture? | No mechanical observations reviewed | Shape alone cannot separate loading, tissue properties, and unloaded geometry |
| Does a finding generalize? | Sources differ in geography and purpose | No independent specimen validation completed |

## Avoid unsupported substitutions

- A taxonomic range is not a probability distribution.
- Anatomical tissue names do not supply constitutive material parameters.
- Properties of processed fiber or dried material would not automatically describe living leaf tissue.
- Repeated descriptions in reference works are not repeated mechanical experiments.
- Successful strawberry modeling does not validate properties of another species.

This is an evidence inventory for botanical understanding. It supplies neither operational removal criteria nor robot interaction parameters.
