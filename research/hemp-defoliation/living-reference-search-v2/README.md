# Living hemp image-reference search v2

Open `index.html` to inspect the verified source improvement. The staged PNG is an original archive member, copied byte-for-byte without a raster transform.

## Ranked sources

1. **APPF TPA phenotyping dataset: UA (Gill) — Hemp (PIA)**. DOI: [10.25909/23211779.v1](https://doi.org/10.25909/23211779.v1). Landing/listing: <https://projects.pawsey.org.au/appf-tpa-0541-ph-ua-gill-hemp-pia/index.html>. License: **CC BY-NC 4.0**. The listing identifies every archive as `[camera_label]_[id_tag].zip` and each archive as a PNG time series. Direct, verified archive: <https://projects.pawsey.org.au/appf-tpa-0541-ph-ua-gill-hemp-pia/RGB_3D_3D_side_far_0_084267-H.zip> (210,403,935 bytes; multipart ETag `9e00e77abb6463419d3a7e3e6ac9a869-26`). The central directory identifies the staged member `RGB_3D_3D_side_far_0_084267-H_2021-02-25_09-08-57.png`, compressed size 6,676,548 bytes, uncompressed size 6,724,916 bytes, local-header offset 203,723,718. Its native dimensions are 2472 × 3296 RGBA; SHA-256 is `d24fb912dd3c6a575302238bbe18cd869b93b4899c925e32711558cec2d5eb63`.

   The public listing has 779 `RGB` archives: `RGB_3D_3D_side_far_[0..5]`, `RGB_3D_3D_side_lower_[0..5]`, `RGB_3D_3D_side_upper[0..5]`, and `RGB_3D_3D_top_0`, across 40 plant IDs. `side_far` is the verified wider-FOV label. Visual review of the staged frame establishes an upright living plant with visible apical tip, main stem, and pot opening; the pot lower edge/bottom is cropped by the image boundary. It also establishes the support cage, sparse elongated form, dark upper foliage, and yellow/dead basal tissue. It does **not** support a green-only foreground claim. No exact cultivar mapping for plant ID `084267-H` was verified. The inherited nominal-cultivar previews are different sample/view/time images, not paired crops; they are narrower derived crops with clipped tips.

2. **Anderson et al. (2021), PLOS ONE Figure 1**. DOI: [10.1371/journal.pone.0252985](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0252985), **CC BY 4.0**. Direct original composite: <https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article/figure/image?size=large&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0252985.g001>, verified 2250 × 2300 PNG. It has dense, mature upright plants, but it is a composite figure rather than a dataset and individual panels are low effective resolution with limited pot context. Candidate anatomy reference only.

3. **Rocamora-Osorio et al. (2025), Automated IoT-Based Monitoring of Industrial Hemp in Greenhouses Using Open-Source Systems and Computer Vision**. Dataset DOI: [10.17632/3md2tbx74c.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/3md2tbx74c.1), **CC BY 4.0**; paper DOI: [10.3390/agriengineering7090272](https://doi.org/10.3390/agriengineering7090272). The dataset explicitly describes hourly images of C1–C5 from transplanting through 20 days. The paper documents Tiborszallasi plants transplanted into pots, front-facing cameras, white panels, and 1080p Full HD capture. However, a direct released C5 sample, `ryb_C5_20241231_17_00.jpg`, is verified at only 273 × 552 pixels. The accessible file list endpoint is <https://data.mendeley.com/public-api/datasets/3md2tbx74c/files?folder_id=08806f60-4306-4f15-8a9d-800f91571221&version=1>. It is a valid living/potted candidate but rejected as a high-resolution replacement.

## Explicit exclusion

Morales et al., *Images and parameters for manuscript on Hemp root and shoot diversity*, [Zenodo 17783092](https://zenodo.org/records/17783092), contains a `ShootCamera_Images.zip`; its record identifies the shoots as camera-taken samples for shoot-size analysis. It does not document living intact potted plants and is excluded.

No additional public dataset found in this bounded search exceeded the APPF native `side_far` match. The 2023 semi-controlled greenhouse study reporting a complete-growth-cycle multispectral time series was found only as a paper abstract; no inspectable public image archive, license, or resolution was located, so it is not a candidate.
