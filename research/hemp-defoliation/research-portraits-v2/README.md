# Single-plant research photographs v2

User requirement: high-quality research photographs explicitly documenting one intact upright potted plant. Native pixel count alone is insufficient.

Selected APPF DroughtSpotter (#0546) specimens 084877-W, 084847-W and 084850-W, with an elevated view of 084847-W at the same timestamp. Native PNGs are 2472 x 3296. No raster transformations or image generation. ZIP members were retrieved using HTTP ranges and decompressed losslessly; SHA256 values in sources.json.

Dataset: https://doi.org/10.25909/23211776.v1
Attribution: Rachel Burton; APPF Plant Accelerator, University of Adelaide; UA (Gill). License verified through Figshare API: CC BY-NC 4.0.

## Visual findings

084877-W is the best whole-shoot candidate: upright, branched, tip visible, no cage. 084847-W offers a simpler stem/leaf arrangement; 084850-W is denser. All have some yellow basal tissue, dark leaves and cropped pot bottoms in the frontal view. The elevated camera shows the full pot but includes imaging apparatus and devotes fewer pixels to the plant. Camera frames have significant black margins. No verified cultivar assignment or production-stage equivalence is claimed.

Rejected Smarthouse (#0541) specimens 084290-H and 084305-H: both clip the shoot tip and contain a blue support cage. Original source https://doi.org/10.25909/23211779.v1 (CC BY-NC 4.0).

LemnaTec public banner https://www.lemnatec.com/wp-content/uploads/LP-Hemp-Phenotyping-2.jpg is 1150 x 400: rejected as a high-resolution replacement. Its pilot study is relevant to controlled multi-view photography, but its web graphics are not original camera frames. https://www.lemnatec.com/hemp-phenotyping/
