What changed and what still needs judgment

Leaf-bearing shoots attach at existing branch nodes and extend in depth as well as across the image. Each added leaf has a petiole and connected leaflets; terminal leaves are smaller. The next visual check is whether the canopy has convincing volume and gaps from several angles. Self-intersections and mechanical properties still need separate validation.
