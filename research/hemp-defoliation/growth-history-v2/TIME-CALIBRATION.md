## Required observation record

For each observation retain source URL/license, specimen ID, cultivar/accession when known, seed/cutting origin, time origin (sowing/emergence/transplant), date or age, scale, camera direction and measured variable. Missing fields stay unknown. Do not merge different specimens into a synthetic longitudinal individual.

## Acceptance before a days label

1. At least two dated observations with a consistent time origin and explicit specimen/cohort provenance.
2. Fit only supported variables; height data does not validate leaf count, density or mechanics.
3. Preserve an unfitted date or individual for comparison when available; otherwise label all comparisons in-sample fitting.
4. Show reference and simulation at matching scale and camera where photographs support it. Report height, width and projected coverage separately.
5. Mark extrapolated ages explicitly; do not infer a commercial production schedule from a research observation window.

## Mechanics remains separate
